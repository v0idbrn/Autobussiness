BAM is a business automation **tool**. You, the operator, are responsible for
using it in compliance with every law, regulation and term of service that
applies to you and your business. This document explains what BAM does and
does not do; it is not legal advice and grants no compliance guarantee.

## Core principles built into BAM

1. **No automatic outreach.** BAM never sends emails, messages or connection
   requests. It generates drafts; a recorded human approval is required before
   any contact is made, and the sending itself is always done by a person.
2. **Discovery ≠ permission.** Finding a company or a publicly published
   contact does not mean you may contact them. A public email address is not
   consent to marketing.
3. **Evidence, not invention.** Claims require captured, hashed evidence.
   BAM does not fabricate company data, problems or personalizations.
4. **Rate limits and robots.txt.** Discovery and research are capped (few
   requests per domain, small page budgets). robots.txt is checked; note that
   when robots.txt cannot be fetched, BAM proceeds under its hard caps — that
   is a technical safeguard, **not** a legal authorization.
5. **Denylist first.** Blocked domains/companies are never researched or
   contacted.

## What you are responsible for

Before contacting anyone, verify at minimum:

- **Anti-spam and e-communications law** in the recipient's jurisdiction
  (e.g. CAN-SPAM, GDPR/ePrivacy in the EU, local equivalents): lawful basis,
  identification, opt-out handling.
- **Terms of service** of any website, platform, search source or API you use
  through or alongside BAM. Sources change their rules and layouts; what is
  permitted today may not be tomorrow.
- **Data protection**: personal data (names, emails, phone numbers, LinkedIn
  URLs) that BAM captures is your responsibility to store, secure, use and
  delete lawfully.
- **Intellectual property**: website content, reports and documents belong to
  their owners. BAM stores short redacted excerpts plus hashes as evidence;
  anything beyond that is your decision and your responsibility.
- **Trade and professional rules** that apply to your offer and delivery.

BAM does **not** guarantee that any particular use is legal in your
jurisdiction or the recipient's. When in doubt, ask a lawyer.

## Third-party sources

- Google News RSS: a public feed; availability, format and acceptable use are
  controlled by Google and may change without notice.
- whois.com WHOIS web view: subject to that site's terms; WHOIS data itself
  may be redacted or restricted by registries and privacy services.
- Any target website: automated access may be limited by its terms, its
  robots.txt, or law.

If a source becomes unavailable or disallows access, do not circumvent it.
BAM is designed to skip and continue — respect that behavior.

## Prohibited uses

- Bulk or unsolicited mass messaging of any kind.
- CAPTCHA, authentication or access-control bypass.
- Scraping at volumes that harm or burden a target.
- Collection of sensitive categories of personal data.
- Any use that violates the law or a platform's terms.
